Recording protocol

Technical controls

  • Hand temperature at least 32 °C
  • Elbow flexed at 90°
  • Measured across-elbow distance 10 cm (BE 4 cm distal, AE 6 cm proximal to the medial epicondyle)
  • Supramaximal stimulation
  • G1 (−) over ADM motor point; G2 (+) per protocol (course notes: little-finger tip; alternative: distal tendon, selectable above); earth around the wrist; wrist stimulation 8 cm from G1
  • Stimulation points: just above the wrist (lateral to FCU), below the medial epicondyle, above and behind the medial epicondyle. Axilla and Erb’s point are not simulated.
  • Distal latency includes the neuromuscular junction (synaptic) delay of about 0.3–1 ms, so at least two stimulation points are needed to calculate conduction velocity.
Interpretation requires clinical context and appropriate technical controls. Thresholds differ between laboratories. NCV = distance (mm) / difference in onset latency (ms) = m/s.
